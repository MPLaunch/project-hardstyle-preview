About

Adelaide's home of hardstyle

Project Hardstyle has been running hardstyle nights in Adelaide since 2014. In their own words: "Bringing ADL the best Hardstyle acts on the PLANET."

International headliners and Adelaide's own residents share the bill, at rooms like Hindley St Music Hall, Lion Arts Factory and Surge. The events, posters and lineups going back to 2015 are in the archive above.
